How it works

From the riverside to your phone in three steps.

  1. Sensors watch the water

    Ultrasonic and float sensors measure the water level in each zone.

  2. The system checks the levels

    When both sensors agree the water is too high, HydroHomie raises an alert.

  3. You get the warning

    Barangay officials are notified and residents can receive text-message alerts.
