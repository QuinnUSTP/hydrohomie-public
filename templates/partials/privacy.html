Who is responsible

What we collect

HydroHomie shows water sensor readings and alerts. These are not personal data. The site has no accounts, forms, comments or advertising, and it does not use cookies or analytics tools.

Technical logs

Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs, such as the time, the page requested and the visitor's IP address, to keep the site running and secure. We use them only for that purpose and do not use them to identify visitors.

If you contact us

If you send us a message, we use your details only to reply and to fix the problem you report. We do not share them or use them for marketing.

Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 you have the right to be informed about, access, correct and object to the use of your personal data, and to complain to the National Privacy Commission.

Changes

If we add features that collect personal data, we will update this notice before we start.
