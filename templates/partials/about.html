What HydroHomie does

HydroHomie gives residents of Barangay Kauswagan a clear, public view of flood conditions in each monitored zone, using sensors placed along local waterways. The goal is to give people more time to prepare and move to safety.

How it works

Sensor nodes in each zone measure the water and send their readings by radio to a barangay computer. That computer checks the readings against the alert levels set by officials, and publishes the latest status to this website.

What it cannot do

Sensors can fail, lose power or read wrongly, and a reading shows one spot rather than your whole street. HydroHomie does not forecast rain or replace official warnings. Use it together with barangay announcements and PAGASA bulletins.
