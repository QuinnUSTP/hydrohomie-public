Before a flood

  • Find out whether your home is in a flood-prone area and how often it floods.
  • Agree on a family plan: who does what, and a meeting place on higher ground.
  • Learn the barangay warning system, your evacuation route and your nearest evacuation center. The Evacuation centers page lists them.
  • Pack a go-bag: drinking water, food, a flashlight and batteries, a radio, a first-aid kit, medicines, copies of IDs in a waterproof bag, and a charged phone or power bank.
  • Keep the numbers on the Emergency hotlines page saved on your phone and written down.
  • Move important papers and valuables to a high place.

When a warning or alert arrives

  • Take it seriously and follow barangay officials' instructions.
  • Leave early if you are told to or if water is rising. Move to higher ground or an evacuation center before the road is cut off.
  • Store clean drinking water and bring belongings and livestock to higher places if you can do it safely.
  • If you have time, turn off the electricity at the main switch before you leave, and lock the house.

During a flood

  • Never walk, swim or drive through floodwater. Even about 15 cm of fast-moving water can knock an adult over.
  • Do not cross a flowing stream if the water is above your knees. Stay away from rivers, canals and bridges covered by water.
  • Keep children away from floodwater and rivers.
  • Stay away from fallen power lines and anything electrical that is wet.
  • Drink only clean or boiled water, and eat only well-cooked food.
  • If you are trapped, go to the highest safe level of the building and call for help.

After a flood

  • Go home only when officials say it is safe.
  • Avoid floodwater and mud. They can carry sewage, chemicals, sharp objects and hidden live wires.
  • Do not switch the power back on until an electrician has checked the wiring. Report broken power, water or gas lines.
  • Throw away food and water that touched floodwater, and boil drinking water until officials say the supply is safe.
  • Wear boots and gloves when cleaning, and use a flashlight instead of candles.
  • See the barangay health center if you or your family feel sick after a flood.

Based on guidance from the PAGASA flood safety guide, the American Red Cross flood safety page and the US ready.gov flood guide.
