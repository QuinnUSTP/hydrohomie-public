What is HydroHomie?

HydroHomie is a flood monitoring system for Barangay Kauswagan. Sensors measure the water in each monitored zone, and this website shows the latest readings so residents can see changing conditions early.

Where do the readings come from?

Each zone has a sensor node. An ultrasonic sensor measures the distance down to the water, and a float switch detects when water reaches a set point. The readings go to a barangay computer, which sends them to this website.

How often does the page update?

The page checks for new data every 15 seconds. Readings usually arrive within about half a minute of being measured. The time under each zone shows when its last reading was taken.

What do Normal and Watch mean?

Normal means the water is below the level the barangay has set for concern. Watch means it has reached that level and conditions may change, so stay alert and follow barangay announcements. Higher alert levels are set by barangay officials and appear on the Alerts and events page.

What does Offline or delayed data mean?

A zone is shown as Offline when no recent reading has arrived. This can be a power cut, a network problem or a damaged sensor. Offline does not mean the water is safe. Check with your barangay and watch conditions yourself.

What does the number in centimeters mean?

It is the estimated height of the water at that sensor above its reference mark. It is not the depth in your street. Use it to see whether the water is rising or falling, and follow official alerts for what to do.

Is this an official warning?

No. HydroHomie supports the barangay's own monitoring and announcements, but it does not replace them. Always follow instructions from barangay officials, PAGASA and the NDRRMC. In an emergency, call 911.

What happens if the power or internet goes out?

If the sensors or the barangay computer lose power or internet, this website cannot get new readings. It will show the last reading with its time, and then mark the zone as Offline. In that case, rely on barangay announcements.

How do I report a wrong reading or a problem?

Use the details on the Contact page. Please tell us the zone, the time you noticed it, and what you expected to see.
